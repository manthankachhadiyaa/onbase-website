Onbase is a platform that connects builders, contractors, and site managers with skilled workers on-demand. You can book workers such as brick masons, carpenters, painters, and electricians for daily jobs, ensuring fast and reliable service.

You can book workers through our app or website. All bookings must be made at least one day in advance so we can assign the right workers to your site.

Pricing is based on a fixed daily rate per worker. You know the cost upfront — no hidden charges or hourly surprises.

Workers come with basic tools. For specialized equipment or materials, you may need to provide them on-site.

Since booking is confirmed one day before, workers arrive the next morning at your site, ready to start the day’s work.

Payments are made securely through our app. You’re charged only after the worker has completed the booked day of work.
